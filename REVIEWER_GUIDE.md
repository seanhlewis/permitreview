# Permit Validation — Reviewer Guide

**What you're doing:** reading a building-permit description and choosing the single category that best
describes the *actual work*. This is a **blind** review — you will **not** see the model's guess. Your job
is to say what the permit really is, in your own judgment, from the text alone.

There are **25 categories**, grouped into **7 families** (Electrical, Mechanical & HVAC, Plumbing,
Exterior, Building, Sitework, Life safety). For each permit the app shows you the 3–5 categories that live
in one family; you pick the right one, or say none of them fit.

---

## How to label — the flow

1. **Enter your name** and pick a task card. Your progress saves automatically; you can stop and resume.
2. For each permit you see **only the description text**. Read it, then either:
   - **Click the category** that matches the main work (or press its number key **1–5**).
     The button flashes **green** to confirm.
   - **"None of these apply" (key 8)** — the work is real but doesn't fit any category shown. This just
     saves *none* and moves on. (If you *know* it belongs to a different family, use the **full-tree**
     escape link to record the correct one.)
   - **"Skip for now" (key 9)** — the text is too vague or cut off to judge. Skipping is fine; don't guess.
3. You can **go back** to earlier permits — your previous choice is highlighted, and you can change it.

**One permit → one category.** If a permit covers several trades, pick the **headline scope** — the single
category that best captures what this permit is primarily for. (E.g. "New single-family residence with
attached garage" → **New construction**, not Garage.)

### Four rules that settle most cases
- **Label the work, not the paperwork.** Ignore the permit *type name*, department, or fee code. A permit
  filed as "Electrical – Misc." that describes a solar array is **Solar PV**.
- **Most specific wins.** Use the broad catch-alls (**Electrical**, **Plumbing**, **Mechanical/HVAC**,
  **Alteration/remodel**) only when no narrower category fits. Solar, EV, battery, panel upgrade, heat pump,
  water heater, etc. always beat the generic parent.
- **When the text won't tell you, Skip** — don't invent detail that isn't written.
- **The electrification categories matter most** (Solar, EV charger, Battery, Panel upgrade, Heat pump).
  Slow down on these and apply the boundary rules below.

### Boundary rules that actually trip people up
| Situation | Correct label |
|---|---|
| **Heat-pump water heater** (domestic hot water) | **Water heater** — *not* Heat pump |
| **Solar thermal / solar hot water** (no electricity) | *not* Solar PV (Solar PV = electric only) |
| **Roof-mounted solar**, no re-roofing scope | **Solar PV** — *not* Roofing |
| Service **capacity change** (100A→200A, meter-main) | **Electrical panel upgrade** — *not* Electrical |
| Any **EV charging equipment** present | **EV charger** — *not* Electrical |
| **Retaining wall / freestanding wall** | **Fence/wall** — *not* Structural |
| **Sewer lateral / water service main** to the street | **Sewer/utility** — *not* Plumbing |
| Interior **fixtures & supply lines** only | **Plumbing** — *not* Sewer/utility |
| **ADU, shed, detached garage, carport** | **Garage/accessory** — *not* New construction |
| **Mini-split / air-source heat pump** | **Heat pump** — *not* Mechanical/HVAC |

## The 25 categories, with a gold-standard example each

Each entry: **what it means**, a **gold example** (a description that is unambiguously this category), and
the **most common thing it is *not***.

### ⚡ Electrical
1. **Solar PV** — Photovoltaic solar *electric* generation equipment.
   - *Gold:* "Install roof-mounted 7.2 kW photovoltaic system, 18 modules with string inverter, residential."
   - *Not:* solar thermal (hot water); a generic electrical permit.
2. **EV charger** — Electric-vehicle charging equipment (EVSE).
   - *Gold:* "Install 240V/40A dedicated circuit and Level 2 EVSE in attached garage."
   - *Not:* parking-area electrical work with no charger.
3. **Battery storage** — Stationary electrical energy storage.
   - *Gold:* "Install Tesla Powerwall (13.5 kWh) battery energy storage system with backup gateway, garage."
   - *Not:* batteries in alarms or vehicles.
4. **Electrical panel upgrade** — Service panel / meter-main replacement or capacity change.
   - *Gold:* "Upgrade main service from 100A to 200A, replace meter-main and load center."
   - *Not:* a reconnect or inspection with no replacement or capacity change.
5. **Electrical** *(catch-all)* — General wiring, circuits, service, fixtures, equipment.
   - *Gold:* "Rewire kitchen, add six 20A circuits, replace outlets, switches and lighting."
   - *Not:* solar / EV / battery / panel upgrade when one of those specifically applies.

### 🔥 Mechanical & HVAC
6. **Heat pump** — Heat-pump equipment (air-source, mini-split, ground-source).
   - *Gold:* "Install 3-ton ducted air-source heat pump to replace gas furnace and AC."
   - *Not:* generic HVAC; a heat-pump *water heater* (that's Water heater).
7. **Mechanical / HVAC** *(catch-all)* — General heating, ventilation, cooling, refrigeration, ductwork.
   - *Gold:* "Replace 4-ton gas furnace and condenser, new ductwork and thermostat; AC changeout."
   - *Not:* a heat pump when that narrower label fits.

### 🚿 Plumbing
8. **Water heater** — Water-heating equipment.
   - *Gold:* "Replace 50-gallon gas water heater with tankless unit; like-for-like."
   - *Not:* a space-heating boiler with no domestic hot water.
9. **Gas** — Fuel-gas piping, meters, or gas-fired equipment connections.
   - *Gold:* "Install new fuel-gas piping and meter for range and dryer; gas line extension."
   - *Not:* water piping; gasoline storage.
10. **Plumbing** *(catch-all)* — General fixtures, water supply, drainage, piping.
    - *Gold:* "Repipe two bathrooms, replace fixtures, water supply and drain lines."
    - *Not:* fuel gas only; a sewer/utility main only.

### 🏠 Exterior
11. **Roofing** — Roof covering replacement, repair, or new roof.
    - *Gold:* "Tear off and reroof 24 squares of asphalt shingles over existing residence."
    - *Not:* roof-mounted equipment with no roofing scope.
12. **Window/door** — Windows, doors, or glazing.
    - *Gold:* "Replace 12 windows and 2 exterior doors like-for-like; retrofit vinyl."
    - *Not:* framing with no window/door/glazing work.
13. **Sign** — Signs and sign structures.
    - *Gold:* "Install illuminated wall sign and monument sign for retail tenant."
    - *Not:* traffic signs; contract signatures.
14. **Deck/patio/porch** — Decks, patios, or porches.
    - *Gold:* "Construct 200 sq ft attached wood deck with stairs at rear of residence."
    - *Not:* an interior floor platform.

### 🏗️ Building
15. **Alteration/remodel** *(catch-all)* — Alteration, renovation, or remodel of an existing building.
    - *Gold:* "Interior remodel of kitchen and two baths, non-structural; tenant improvement."
    - *Not:* a new standalone building with no alteration scope.
16. **New construction** — A new principal building or structure.
    - *Gold:* "Construct new two-story single-family residence, 2,400 sq ft."
    - *Not:* an addition or accessory structure only.
17. **Demolition** — Full or partial demolition.
    - *Gold:* "Full demolition of existing single-family residence and foundation."
    - *Not:* removing one fixture with no demolition scope.
18. **Garage/accessory** — Garage, carport, shed, ADU, or other accessory structure.
    - *Gold:* "Construct new detached 400 sq ft garage" — or — "Build 600 sq ft accessory dwelling unit (ADU)."
    - *Not:* a principal new building.
19. **Structural** — Framing, foundation, load-bearing or reinforcement work.
    - *Gold:* "Foundation repair: install 8 steel piers and reinforce floor framing; seismic retrofit."
    - *Not:* non-structural finish work.

### 🌳 Sitework
20. **Pool/spa** — Swimming pool, spa, hot tub, or pool equipment.
    - *Gold:* "Construct in-ground gunite swimming pool and spa with equipment pad."
    - *Not:* a decorative fountain or a commercial beauty spa.
21. **Fence/wall** — Fences or freestanding/retaining walls.
    - *Gold:* "Install 120 ft of 6 ft cedar fence and a 4 ft masonry retaining wall."
    - *Not:* an interior partition wall.
22. **Driveway/paving** — Driveway, sidewalk, paving, or hardscape.
    - *Gold:* "Replace concrete driveway approach and sidewalk; new asphalt paving."
    - *Not:* roof paving products.
23. **Grading/sitework** — Earthwork, excavation, grading, erosion control, site prep.
    - *Gold:* "Rough grading and excavation with erosion control for site preparation."
    - *Not:* an interior renovation.
24. **Sewer/utility** — Sewer/water service main, lateral, or right-of-way utility connection.
    - *Gold:* "Replace sewer lateral from house to city main" — or — "New 1-inch water service line."
    - *Not:* interior plumbing fixtures.

### 🧯 Life safety
25. **Fire protection** — Fire alarm, sprinkler, suppression, or detection systems.
    - *Gold:* "Install NFPA 13D fire sprinkler system throughout new residence; monitored fire alarm."
    - *Not:* a lawn irrigation sprinkler.

## Quick reference — the whole decision in one line
> Read the description → pick the **most specific** category for the **main work** → if nothing in the
> shown family fits, **None of these apply** → if you can't tell, **Skip**. Watch the electrification
> boundaries (heat-pump water heater = Water heater; roof-mount solar = Solar PV; capacity change = Panel
> upgrade). Label the **work**, never the paperwork.
