Review guide

Choose a category and review the permits shown in that queue. Read the description and supporting metadata before selecting Yes, No, or Unclear.

Starting a queue does not claim a permit. The permit is associated with your first saved answer. Your first name initial appears beside the permit for this browser.

The live server keeps two reviewer slots per category permit and locks a category after two reviewers save their first answers. Reviewer initials are visible to everyone using this server.

Comments autosave to the shared database as you type. Use the Previous and Next controls or the arrow keys to move through the queue.
