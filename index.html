How to review this round

Read the permit description and choose the most specific active category supported by the text. The model prediction is hidden during this review.

Before you start

  1. Enter your reviewer name in the top bar.
  2. Use the active targeted queues. Each card shows your assigned count and remaining count.
  3. Historical cards are locked. Their old records remain preserved.

Assignment rule: the system targets at least 10 permits per available active class, caps each reviewer at 300 class-target reviews, and allows no more than two substantive reviewers per class-target permit. Demolition, Sign, and Window / door have fewer than 10 permits in this round. Solar PV is not an active queue. Heat Pump appears as a choice inside the Mechanical / HVAC queue when the description supports it.

For each permit

  1. Read the full description.
  2. Choose the narrow category that matches the main work. One permit can appear in multiple class queues because one permit can support multiple class-specific estimates.
  3. Use None of these apply when the work is real and no shown category fits.
  4. Use Skip for now when the description is too vague, incomplete, or contradictory.
  5. Use the note field for a short explanation. Notes and unfinished selections autosave after a short pause. Autosave does not complete the permit.

Category selection completes the permit. You can use Back to revisit a saved choice. Forward saves any unfinished note before moving.

Reviewer guide

How to label a permit

You read a building-permit description and choose the single category that best describes the actual work. This is a blind review — you will not see the model's guess. Say what the permit really is, from the text alone.

The flow

  1. Click the category that matches the main work, or press its number key 1–5. The button flashes green to confirm.
  2. None of these apply (8) — the work is real but fits no category shown. Saves none and moves on. If you know it belongs to another family, use the full-tree escape link to record it.
  3. Skip for now (9) — the text is too vague or cut off to judge. Skipping is fine — don't guess.
  4. You can go back: your previous choice is highlighted and can be changed.

One permit → one category. If it covers several trades, pick the headline scope — e.g. “New residence with attached garage” is New construction, not Garage.

Four rules that settle most cases

  • Label the work, not the paperwork. Ignore the permit type name, department, or fee code. A permit filed “Electrical – Misc.” describing a solar array is Solar PV.
  • Most specific wins. Use the catch-alls (Electrical, Plumbing, Mechanical/HVAC, Alteration/remodel) only when no narrower category fits.
  • When the text won't tell you, Skip — don't invent detail.
  • The electrification categories matter most (Solar, EV charger, Battery, Panel upgrade, Heat pump). Slow down and apply the boundary rules.

The 25 categories — with a gold-standard example each

Each shows what it means, a gold example (unambiguously this category), and the most common thing it is not.

⚡ Electrical

1 · Solar PV

Photovoltaic solar electric generation equipment.

“Install roof-mounted 7.2 kW photovoltaic system, 18 modules with string inverter, residential.”

Not: solar thermal; a generic electrical permit.

2 · EV charger

Electric-vehicle charging equipment (EVSE).

“Install 240V/40A dedicated circuit and Level 2 EVSE in attached garage.”

Not: parking electrical with no charger.

3 · Battery storage

Stationary electrical energy storage.

“Install Powerwall (13.5 kWh) battery energy storage system with backup gateway, garage.”

Not: batteries in alarms or vehicles.

4 · Panel upgrade

Service panel / meter-main replacement or capacity change.

“Upgrade main service from 100A to 200A, replace meter-main and load center.”

Not: reconnect/inspection with no change.

5 · Electrical catch-all

General wiring, circuits, service, fixtures, equipment.

“Rewire kitchen, add six 20A circuits, replace outlets, switches and lighting.”

Not: solar/EV/battery/panel when one applies.

🔥 Mechanical & HVAC

6 · Heat pump

Heat-pump equipment (air-source, mini-split, ground-source).

“Install 3-ton ducted air-source heat pump to replace gas furnace and AC.”

Not: generic HVAC; a heat-pump water heater.

7 · Mechanical / HVAC catch-all

General heating, ventilation, cooling, refrigeration, ductwork.

“Replace 4-ton gas furnace and condenser, new ductwork and thermostat; AC changeout.”

Not: a heat pump when that fits.

“Replace 50-gallon gas water heater with tankless unit; like-for-like.”

“Install new fuel-gas piping and meter for range and dryer; gas line extension.”

Not: water piping; gasoline storage.

10 · Plumbing catch-all

General fixtures, water supply, drainage, piping.

“Repipe two bathrooms, replace fixtures, water supply and drain lines.”

Not: fuel gas only; a sewer main only.

The whole decision in one line

Read the description → pick the most specific category for the main work → if nothing in the shown family fits, None of these apply → if you can't tell, Skip. Watch the electrification boundaries. Label the work, never the paperwork.
